What We Do

Survey & Mapping Services

Precise, fast turnaround services for residential builds, subdivisions, and commercial sites.

🚁

Drone & Aerial Mapping

High-resolution aerial capture for site planning, progress tracking, and volumetric analysis.

  • Orthomosaic & 3D site models
  • Stockpile & cut/fill volume reports
  • Progress monitoring for builders
📐

Topographic Surveys

Detailed ground-level data for design, drainage, and engineering plans.

  • Contours & digital terrain models
  • Feature & level surveys
  • Fast delivery for design teams
📍

Construction Staking & Set-Out

On-site marking so builders and contractors can start work with confidence.

  • Building & structure set-out
  • Slab, footing & pier marking
  • As-built verification surveys
Please note: Sunshine Surveyors provides drone mapping, topographic, and construction set-out/as-built services only. We do not perform legal boundary/title (cadastral) surveys, which in Victoria can only be carried out by a Licensed Surveyor registered with the Surveyors Registration Board of Victoria. Need a boundary survey? Ask us for a referral.
