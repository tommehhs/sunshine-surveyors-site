# Roadmap

**Direction: Open** (Tommy unsure as of 2026-10-05). No build work until this is decided.

## The options

### A. Own services
Keep the current site. Sell drone, topo, set-out, as-builts and TS15 dry hire, done by Tommy.
- Cheap and fast; the site already exists.
- Limited by Tommy's time alongside his full-time job.

### B. Lead broker (the July 2026 spec)
Capture cadastral search traffic ("boundary survey Sunshine") and pass the jobs to a licensed partner for a fee.
- Needs a partner signed first (SMC Land Surveyors was the target).
- Bigger build: Next.js + Supabase, ~80 suburb × service pages.
- Earns per lead without doing the survey work; could scale.
- Clashes with the live site's "we don't do boundary surveys" positioning, so the copy would change.

### C. Both, in sequence
Run A now on the current site. Build B later once a partner is in place, likely as new pages or a rebuild.

## Phases (once a direction is chosen)
1. **Foundations:** domains paid up, email and phone working, form notifications tested, repo private.
2. **Get found:** Google Business Profile, Search Console, sitemap submitted.
3. **First revenue:** first quote converted, or first TS15 dry hire.
4. **Scale (option B or C):** partner agreement, then the programmatic suburb pages.

## Out of scope for now
- Town planning reports (planned in the July spec as a later, switched-off category)
- Paid ads
