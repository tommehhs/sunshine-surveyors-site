# Competitor notes (July 2026)

Reviewed while writing the July build spec. Kept here so the research isn't repeated.

| Competitor | Take-away |
|---|---|
| **Landtek Group** | Strong conversion layout: quote form high on the page, tappable phone, hours shown, fixed pricing, fast turnaround promise. Relies on paid ads; no suburb-level SEO pages. |
| **JAC Surveyors** | Credibility structure: services described separately from projects that prove the work. |
| **Planna** | Venture-backed town planning tech. Bids on "land surveyor" ads in Victoria and routes survey work to partners. Proves the lead-broker model. |
| **SMC Land Surveyors** | Sunshine-based licensed firm; good reputation, weak website. Partner target, not just a competitor. |
| **Prime Land Consultants** | Frames the whole approval journey (survey + planning). Backup partner. |
| **Measure Australia**, **Flat Out Surveying** | Also reviewed; no unique take-aways recorded. |

## Gap identified
Competitors buy clicks; none has hyperlocal suburb pages with real local data. That was the planned edge for the lead-broker model.
